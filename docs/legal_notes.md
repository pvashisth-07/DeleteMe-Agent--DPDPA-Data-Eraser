## 2. Notification & Commencement Timeline (G.S.R. 843(E))

The Central Government has notified a staggered implementation timeline:
1. **On Notification**: Sections 1(2), 2, 18–26 (Establishment of the Data Protection Board), 35, 38–43, 44(1),(3).
2. **+1 Year**: Section 6(9), Section 27(1)(d).
3. **+18 Months**: Sections 3–5, 6(1)–(8),(10), 7–10, **Sections 11–17 (Rights of Data Principals including s.12 Erasure)**, 27 (except (1)(d)), 28–34, 36, 37, 44(2).

### Architectural Implementation:
- **`LAW_IN_FORCE=false`**: Because substantive rights (s.12) are scheduled +18 months from commencement, our default notice template uses **pre-commencement, good-faith cooperation phrasing**:
  > *"I am writing to formally request the complete erasure of my personal data associated with my account, in proactive alignment with Section 12 of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) and the DPDP Rules 2025..."*
- If configured with `LAW_IN_FORCE=true`, notices immediately switch to formal statutory demand phrasing citing post-commencement penalties.

## 4. Grievance Escalation & Data Protection Board Invariants

1. **Section 13(3) Exhaustion**: The Tracker automatically validates that an initial 30-day notice and a follow-up notice have elapsed with no substantive resolution before generating an escalation complaint.
2. **Zero Board Fee Invariant**: Neither the Act nor the DPDP Rules specify a filing fee for Data Principals registering a grievance with the Board. **DeleteMe Agent strictly never states, assumes, or charges a Board filing fee.**
3. **Never Auto-File**: Legal filings before judicial or regulatory bodies carry legal liabilities. The agent prepares the complete evidentiary brief, timeline, and relief sought as a structured draft for the human consumer. It **never** auto-submits filings.
4. **Statutory Disclaimer**: Every communication generated by DeleteMe contains the mandatory disclaimer:
   > *"Notice: This is general information, not legal advice."*
